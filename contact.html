FAQ

Before you write to us

How far in advance should I book?

For peak season — spring and autumn weekends — we recommend 3 to 6 months. Smaller orders can often be turned around in days, and we handle last-minute requests whenever inventory allows.

Do you deliver, set up and break down?

Yes. Every order includes delivery, placement to your floor plan and teardown after the event. We coordinate load-in windows directly with your venue.

Can I see items before I book?

Absolutely. Browse the rentals catalogue and the gallery, then ask us for linen swatches or a showroom visit in your city.

What happens if the weather turns?

We'll build a rain plan with you — tents, sidewalls, heating or an indoor alternative — and set a clear rain-call deadline, normally 24 to 48 hours out.

Do you work with planners and caterers?

Constantly. We partner with leading planners, caterers, designers and production teams, and we're happy to coordinate directly with your vendors.
